# GlowForge AI

Redesign this portfolio into a high-end, futuristic, and sleek AI UGC Creator & Social Media Marketer portfolio.

1. Aesthetic & Theme:
- Dark Neo-Brutalist / Glassmorphism hybrid style.
- Background: Deep slate/obsidian (#0A0D14) with subtle radial gradients (electric indigo and emerald glow).
- Typography: Clean sans-serif for body (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans) and bold modern display font for titles (Syne, Clash Display, or Space Grotesk).
- Card Style: Frosted glass effect (bg-white/5 border border-white/10 backdrop-blur-md rounded-2xl).

2. Hero Section:
- Top badge: Glowing pill tag with "Available for Brand Campaigns • 2026".
- Headline: "AI-Powered UGC Ads That Stop The Scroll & Scale Brands."
- Sub-headline: "I create high-performing creator-style video ads, studio-grade product visuals, and social growth strategies that convert viewers into buyers."
- CTA Buttons: Primary glow button "View Selected Work" + Secondary outlined button "Book a Project via WhatsApp".
- Hero Visual: An interactive 3D-tilted or floating 9:16 smartphone mockup displaying a looping UGC video ad with a subtle glowing border.

3. Selected Work Section (Portfolio Grid):
- Filter tags: "All", "Skincare & Beauty", "Fashion & Apparel", "Food & Beverage", "Tech & DTC".
- Grid: 3-column layout featuring vertical 9:16 smartphone cards.
- Card details:
  * Crisp rounded phone frames with a sleek border.
  * Autoplay video preview / smooth zoom on hover.
  * Tag overlay for niche (e.g., "Skincare Routine Ad", "Meta & Reels Ready").
  * Metrics badge on card corner (e.g., "+3.8x ROAS", "150k+ Views", "Viral Hook").

4. Services & Capabilities (Bento Grid):
- Card 1 (Large): "AI UGC Video Production" - High-energy, authentic creator-style videos built with advanced AI workflows without hiring expensive physical talent.
- Card 2: "Virtual Product Photography" - Studio-quality product shots in aesthetic lifestyle environments.
- Card 3: "Social Media Strategy" - Viral hook writing, content calendar orchestration, and paid ad creative testing.
- Card 4: "Speed & Scale" - Fast turnaround, A/B test variations, and platform-specific formatting.

5. About & Credibility:
- Two-column split: Left side with a sharp, professional creator visual/profile card and quick stats (100% Remote, Fast Delivery, Direct Communication). Right side with punchy, bulleted value propositions highlighting brand growth and cost-efficient scaling.

6. High-Converting Contact Section:
- Headline: "Let’s build your next top-performing ad campaign."
- Left side: Direct contact badges (Email: kushkumarofficial06@gmail.com, WhatsApp quick-link).
- Right side: Clean minimal input form (Name, Brand Website/Product Link, Project Goal, Budget range selector).
- Smooth toast notification on submit.
